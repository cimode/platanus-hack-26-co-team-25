# PILLARS — The compatibility instrument's module taxonomy

> Derived from `RESEARCH-COMPATIBILITY.md` through a 10-agent adversarial workflow:
> four independent taxonomies (relationship science / psychometrics / product / lens
> differentiation) → synthesis → four adversarial passes (evidence integrity, factor
> redundancy, measurability, safety & demo) → finalisation.
> **6 fatal and 21 serious verdicts were raised and resolved.** Subsequently audited by
> `AUDIT.md` (7-agent transversal review); its corrections are applied inline below.
> Last updated: 2026-08-22.

---

## 0. The headline

**Ten pillars. Four of them are measured. Six cost nothing.**

The scarce resource is not ideas, it is **blocks**. At 12–15 forced-choice blocks the
instrument can estimate roughly four latent traits to 2–3 distinguishable bands each —
and nothing more. Everything else has to arrive through a channel that doesn't cost a
block: a declared fact, an observed piece of room structure, or a governance rule.

That ratio — **40% latent, 60% free** — is the finding, not an accident. Three separate
adversarial passes converged on it independently. A taxonomy shaped like its constraints
is the honest one.

## 1. Admission criteria

*This is the classification machinery: how a candidate dimension earns pillar status, and
how it gets assigned its mathematical role. The criteria matter more than the list.*

| # | Criterion | The test |
|---|---|---|
| **A1** | **One metric, one lower bound, or zero weight.** Every scored weight traces to a published coefficient on a **common observed-r metric**, set at the lower bound of its interval. A rank, a description, or a trade book may *motivate* a term; it may never set a magnitude. | State study, N, sample, DV, coefficient, correction status, CI lower bound. De-correct any ρ. Portfolio audit: unanchored weight mass ≤ .15 per lens unless concentrated in one named ablatable parameter. |
| **A2** | **Declare the outcome class.** Two weight vectors — `w_rank` (**formation**) and `w_sim` (**quality**) — and the transfer between them is **one named, ablatable parameter per lens**, never a pile of per-pillar guesses. | Is the source sample strangers or established dyads? Assign `w_rank` and `w_sim` separately. They routinely differ by an order of magnitude in **both** directions. |
| **A3** | **Term type is derived from relational structure, then must clear the Joel-2020 dyadic burden of proof.** Maximum **four dyadic terms** per lens. | External performance criterion → level with soft-min. Resource under Equality Matching → similarity. Role under Authority Ranking → penalise high-high. Discontinuous + irreversible + costly exit → gate. Opportunity not trait → saturating overlap. Then: absent published evidence that the *dyadic combination* explains variance beyond both main effects, downgrade to level or narrative. |
| **A4** | **Error budget.** The instrument delivers 2–3 distinguishable bands per latent. Every term type must be legible at 3 bands. **No *continuous* `\|a−b\|` similarity on a latent, anywhere. No hard multiplicative gate on a latent, ever.** | Simulate the term at SE ≈ .45. If it inverts, saturates, or produces a nonsense argmax, it is deleted rather than downweighted. Sole admitted exception (`AUDIT.md` S6): **banded** difference penalties computed as `P(gap ≥ k)` from the posteriors — like the high-high penalty — which must pass the same SE ≈ .45 simulation. |
| **A5** | **Channel test: DECLARE, OBSERVE, INFER — in that order.** Only a *socially desirable latent under faking pressure* may spend a block. | If a tap gets it, a tap gets it. Then run collinearity **symmetrically** and audit duplication by **construct**, not by latent name. |
| **A6** | **Constructibility in *this* room.** Desirability-matched, negatively-keyable, and domain-safe. | A latent whose high pole is a **competence flex in the room where it is administered** cannot be measured there, regardless of effect size. |
| **A7** | **Read-aloud safety**, enforced by three machine-readable fields on every pillar: `displayClass`, `narrativeAuthority`, `dataClass`. | **Safety overrides effect size.** No exceptions, including for our best predictor. |
| **A8** | **Asking is a disclosure event**, scored independently of display. | A question a person would not want screenshotted fails **even if the answer is never shown**. |
| **A9** | **Discrimination, three ways**: across people in this room, across lenses, and against the room's restricted range. | Applied to the pillars we like, not only to the ones we were already cutting. |
| **A10** | **Every pillar specifies its degraded mode.** | If "what does this term do at zero data" answers "it is silently null", the pillar has no degraded mode and the ranking will lie at partial completion. |
| **A11** | **Narrative yield last**, as a tiebreaker only, measured in observed choices rather than latents. | A pillar never enters on storytelling value alone. |

## 2. The ten pillars

### Measured — 4 latents, 15 blocks

| Pillar | Term type | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Regulation** | level (soft-min) | How much distress a person generates and carries in, and how fast they return to baseline. Absorbs attachment anxiety **and** conflict flooding. Measured in **non-work life domains only**. |
| **Politeness** | level (soft-min) | Characteristic restraint from contempt, criticism and derogation. Big Five agreeableness narrowed to its Politeness aspect; Compassion cut; circumplex affiliation measured here **once** rather than twice. |
| **Reliability** | level (soft-min → hard min in business) | Follow-through on stated commitments under boredom and adversity. Orderliness removed to the declared channel. |
| **Agency** | anti-complementarity | Who takes the wheel when it is genuinely unclear who should, and whether being overruled is survivable. Scoped strictly to dyadic decision rights. |

### Free — 6 pillars at zero block cost

| Pillar | Channel | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Distance & Re-initiation** | declared bands | How long you stay away after closeness or conflict, and whether you are the one who comes back. Two retrospective bands, not a latent. |
| **Life Shape & Capacity** | declared | Money posture, rootedness, family gravity — and **discretionary hours actually spent in the last four weeks**. Retrospective fact, not future intention. |
| **Common Ground** | declared tags | Interests, media, food, activity, pets, chronotype. Its job is to **manufacture perceived similarity**, not to score the pair. |
| **Structural Proximity & Introduction** | observed | Team membership and adjacency, track, form-submission timestamp as arrival cohort, capped declared acquaintance list. The only pillar whose output is an **intervention**, not a number. |
| **Eligibility Gates** | declared, server-only | The small set of absolute binary facts that decide whether a pairing is real at all. The only pillar with genuinely lens-partitioned content. |
| **Consent & Disclosure Control** | governance | What may be computed, what may be shown, and to whom. Zero scoring weight, zero narrative yield — admitted because **its failure mode is a class of harm no scored pillar can prevent**. |

## 3. The two weight vectors

`w_rank` ranks the room (a **formation** problem — strangers). `w_sim` drives the timeline
and hazard model (a **quality** problem — established dyads). Almost every effect size in
the literature is a *quality* study. Running one vector for both would rank the room by
predicted marital satisfaction, which is not the decision anyone in that room is making.

**The τ transfer parameter** *(restated per `AUDIT.md` S1)*. τ is the total
**trait-derived** contribution to `w_rank` — the four latents **plus** the declared
Distance bands, none of which has a formation anchor: **τ = .35 romance / .34 business /
.17 friendship**, apportioned in proportion to quality coefficients, because those are the
only numbers that exist. The four *latents alone* sum to **.27 / .34 / .11** — the earlier
label "total latent contribution" was wrong by the tables above. τ is ablatable at 0 and
0.5, both pre-rendered for the pitch. Consequence, stated rather than hidden: the
**anchored fraction of `w_rank` is 65% / 66% / 83%**. The friendship ranking is 83%
opportunity and declared overlap, **11% inferred latents and 6% declared re-contact
bands** — which is exactly what Festinger, Back, Kossinets and Hall say, and it is the
strongest evidence base in the model.

## 4. The four inversions

Where a pillar's mathematical form changes across lenses — the structural claim that makes
this a designed model rather than a reweighted one.

1. **Distance & Re-initiation** — soft-min level in romance · **zero** in business · **max()** in friendship. Friendships almost never end in conflict; they end by attrition. After three months of silence, one texter is enough.
2. **Agency** — both-high penalty in romance and business · **band-gap** penalty in friendship. Under Authority Ranking a dominance gap is an org chart; under Equality Matching it is patronage.
3. **Life Shape & Capacity** — steep graded **gap penalty** in business · soft **similarity** in romance · **level + window overlap** in friendship. One declared variable, three mathematical forms. A full-time founder paired with a nights-and-weekends founder is the canonical resentment engine.
4. **Homophily** — structural proximity positive in all three lenses; **demographic** homophily refused as a scored business input and retained only as an audit slate. Ruef documents that founders already over-select on surface homophily; scoring it would automate the bias.

> **Both surviving complementarity-descended geometries (the Agency forms) are penalties —
> and no term anywhere in this model rewards a difference.** We lose the elegant "opposites attract *and* birds of a feather, on different
> axes" line — see §7 — and gain a defensible one.

## 5. What was cut, and on which criterion

- **A1 (no coefficient / wrong metric)** — the business Regulation anchor; the business agreeableness inverted-U; Distance's business weight; every normative sentence built on Ruef 2003 (an outcome-free descriptive study).
- **A3 (dyadic burden)** — the Four Horsemen as four measured dimensions; the derived task-vs-relationship conflict interaction.
- **A4 (error budget)** — the symmetric complementarity form; every `|a−b|` similarity term on a latent; the continuous anxious × avoidant product.
- **A5 (channel)** — attachment anxiety as its own latent (merged into Regulation); circumplex affiliation as a separate row (a rotation of the Politeness plane — the original table paid for it **twice**); Orderliness; Social Energy / extraversion; humor as a latent; Openness as a latent; **withdrawal as a latent entirely**.
- **A6 (constructibility)** — anything whose high pole is a competence flex in an engineering room.
- **A7/A8 (safety)** — monogamy and relationship structure; children *timing*; moral, religious and political orientation; substances as a public tag; life satisfaction as a measured dimension; the Four Horsemen words in any template; mutual-acquaintance counts and identities.
- **A9** — raw ambition-vs-balance (range restriction); demographic homophily as a scored input.
- **A10** — shared table, live co-location, check-in cohort, social-graph import: no degraded mode, infrastructure doesn't exist.

**Named exclusions rather than quiet omissions:**

- **Facial attractiveness from the photos we already hold.** It passes every scientific criterion — Joel 2017 puts partner variance at 7–27%, the most predictable component of romantic desire (vs 4–18% actor) — and it fails **A7** outright. It is the most predictive feature available to us and we deliberately do not build it.
- **Joel 2020's top tier** — perceived partner commitment, appreciation, sexual satisfaction, conflict — up to 45% of variance, and **not unilaterally estimable before the relationship exists**. Naming that exclusion *is* the honest version of "we simulate, we do not predict."

## 6. Known weaknesses

1. **The friendship lens has no quality evidence at all.** Every friendship citation in the corpus — Festinger, Back, Kossinets & Watts, Hall, Selfhout — has a formation or tie-existence DV. Not one measures friendship *quality* or survival. Consequence, taken as a real product cut: **the friendship lens ships no survival curve.** It gets a timeline, and makes no numeric duration claim.
2. **Regulation is now the most heterogeneous latent in the set**, carrying ambiguity tolerance, reactivity, relational threat sensitivity and conflict flooding. Moving shutdown in was necessary to decontaminate Distance, but a technical judge is entitled to ask whether one θ is doing four jobs. Regulation, Politeness and Reliability also share the Stability metatrait — three of four latents load a general factor before an item is written.
3. **τ is the largest unanchored quantity in the model.** It is one number, it is named, and it is ablatable — which is the best available position, not a good one.

## 7. Two corrections to earlier documents

**7.1 — The complementarity formula in `RESEARCH-COMPATIBILITY.md` §4.1 is wrong and is
withdrawn.** The form `1 − exp(−Δ²/2σ²)` **ranks a maximally submissive person as the ideal
match for a maximally dominant one**, monotonically, forever. It is deleted rather than
downweighted. Agency is now an **anti-complementarity** term: a penalty on the high-high
corner, computed as `P(both in top band)` from the posteriors × a fixed penalty, with the
probability displayed. In friendship it inverts to a band-gap penalty. Published evidence
for dominance-complementarity *benefits* exists (Dryer & Horowitz 1997; Markey & Markey
2007) and is deliberately left unexploited: at 2–3 bands of resolution a reward term is
not legible, so the penalty-only form is kept on error-budget (A4) grounds — not because
no positive evidence exists.

**7.2 — Adaptive item selection is dropped from the pitch.** Fifteen authored blocks
administered as fifteen blocks is a **fixed balanced form**; there is nothing to select
from. Real adaptivity needs a bank of 40–60 blocks — 160–240 authored, rated, validated
options, three to four times the critical path — and it would destroy the balanced design
whose linking properties the precision claim rests on. We ship the fixed form, we say so,
and we defend it on the combinatorics, which are genuinely elegant: with D = 4 latents and
blocks of 4, every block loads all four latents, so every latent appears in 15 of 15 blocks
and every latent *pair* **co-occurs** in all 15 — most+least elicitation observes 5 of 6
pairwise orderings per block, so every pair is *directly compared* ~12–13 times in
expectation (`AUDIT.md` S5). Linking is maximal, and linking legitimizes between-person
**contrasts**; it is **reversed keying** (§8, rule 1) that legitimizes the *level* terms
the model leans on. **Claiming both fixed-form rigour and adaptivity is the overclaim a
technical judge catches.**

## 8. Immediate consequence for the build

**Structural Proximity carries the largest ranking weight in two of three lenses (.30
friendship, .24 business), and two of its inputs must be form fields.** Team membership and
track have to be captured at intake. That decision precedes the form shipping — and the
form is the first deliverable (`CONTEXT.md` §4). **Block authoring is today's second
deliverable** — the form *contains* the 15 blocks, so the rules below ship with it
(`AUDIT.md` F1, S13).

**Hard build rules (from `AUDIT.md` — non-negotiable):**

1. **Mixed keying is mandatory: at least one reversed-keyed option per block, rotated
   across traits** (F1). With every block loading the same four traits and all options
   positively keyed, the forced-choice likelihood is invariant to a common shift in trait
   levels — all 60 options carry **zero information about levels**, the exact direction
   the soft-min terms consume. Simulated recovery: ~.19 level correlation all-positive;
   **.93–.95 with one reversed-keyed option per block**. Irreversible once the form ships.
2. **Band cutoffs are fixed a-priori** on the latent scale from the assumed loadings,
   frozen before the first response, never re-banded after display (S14). No within-room
   percentile norming — at N ≈ 20 it flips band memberships as responses arrive.
3. **Degraded mode, decided now** (S15): a missing latent imputes to the population prior
   mean; weights never renormalize; profiles below the floor (no gates or no photo) are
   excluded from the reveal rather than ranked.
4. **Photo + per-lens consent move to the front of the flow** with their own minute (S16);
   if completions are under 15 by the agreed trigger hour, execute the cut order. Show
   each respondent their own result immediately on completion.

**Intake shape:** declared round first (~26 taps + one tag picker, 2.5–3.5 min), then 15
blocks (4–6 min). Total 6.5–9.5 minutes (`AUDIT.md` S16). The declarative round runs first because it is demo
insurance — someone who abandons at block 4 still has gates, tags, capacity and proximity,
still ranks, and still renders a panel. Cut order if completion binds: drop most+least
elicitation → 15 blocks down to 12 → tag picker 30 down to 12. **Never cut consent, gates,
or Life Shape.**
